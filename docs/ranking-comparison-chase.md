## Versus the flowchart
Flowchart personal order: Sapphire, United (Quest, Explorer, Club), Aeroplan, Southwest (Priority, Plus), Marriott Boundless, IHG Premier, Hyatt, Avios, Freedom. Flowchart business order: Ink, United Business, Hyatt Business (no longer offered), Southwest Business, IHG Business.

- Business tracks well: Ink Preferred, United Business, Southwest, IHG all in roughly the flowchart order. The new Sapphire Reserve Business tops the list and is not in the flowchart.
- Personal diverges: United (flowchart 2nd) lands 9th to 16th; Aeroplan (3rd) is 1st; Southwest (4th) is 7th to 8th, since the Companion Pass is not valued; Marriott Boundless (5th) is 3rd; IHG (6th) is 5th; Hyatt (7th) drops to 14th, consistent with the flowchart's note that Hyatt has had no good bonus in years; Avios (8th) is 4th; Freedom is last in both.
- Not visible to the formula: Companion Pass, minimum-spend feasibility (Sapphire Reserve Business needs $30,000 in 6 months), the cost of a 5/24 slot, Chase's 3-month spacing.
