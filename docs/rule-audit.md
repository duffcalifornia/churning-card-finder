## H. Marriott eligibility matrix (replaces F20 and E3)

Source: https://frequentmiler.com/marriott-bonvoy-complete-guide/#Credit_Card_Eligibility (page updated 2026-02-09). Decoded from the page's table structure, including its icon cells. Consistent with the flowchart's Marriott paths (24 months after Chase bonuses for Amex cards; Bevy needs Chase Boundless/Bountiful cancelled plus 30 days). Status: unverified until the user confirms.

Key (what a prior card does to a card you want):
- OK: no restriction.
- 30: no welcome bonus if you had the prior card within the past 30 days.
- 24: not approved if you currently have the card, or received its welcome bonus in the past 24 months.
- 90+24: no bonus if approved for the prior card within 90 days, or received a welcome/upgrade bonus in the past 24 months.
- 30+90+24: all three above apply.
- EVER: no bonus if you have ever had the prior card (the system seems to forget after about 5 to 7 years).

## J. Amex family rules (replaces the family parts of A11, F7, G7)

Source: user, 2026-09-20. Status: confirmed by user; card tier lists below are the source of truth for the engine.

Rule: within a family, if you have EVER had a card in a higher tier, you cannot get the welcome bonus on a card in a lower tier. Exception: NLL offers (offers without the family language) generally exclude the family restriction. Cards on the same tier (slash-separated) do not block each other's bonus by this rule; each blocks all lower tiers.

Tiers are listed lowest to highest (lowest tier first). A card is blocked by having had any card in a higher tier.

Engine design for NLL (decision, user, 2026-09-20):
- Cards blocked only by an Amex family rule (Section J) or the once-per-lifetime rule (A10, E4) do NOT appear in the normal ranked results.
- If the user's card history shows they have had a blocking card in the family, or the same card before, the results page adds a separate section, "Only if you have an NLL offer", listing those cards, since an NLL offer would remove the block.
- The section appears only when it applies (the user has such history and at least one card in the catalog is blocked that way). Each entry says why it is blocked and that it is only worth applying for with an NLL offer, which the site cannot check.
- This replaces the earlier idea of a per-card "I have an NLL offer" toggle in the card history input (E4).
